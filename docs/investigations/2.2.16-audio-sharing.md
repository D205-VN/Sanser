# 2.2.16 audio and group-video investigation

Date: 2026-10-18. Development branch: `Version-2.0.0`.

The user confirmed both computers run 2.2.16. Windows controlling Mac means
Mac capture → Windows playback (crackling). Mac controlling Windows means
Windows capture → Mac playback (missing sound). This distinction matters because
the latter still uses the legacy launcher/media path.

## Findings and changes

| Symptom | Confirmed source defect | Change | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mac controlling Windows has no sound | Host launcher omitted `--audio-udp-connect`; Mac client started audio only for a nonzero dedicated port, although port zero selects multiplexed audio | Opt capture into the main UDP endpoint; start the multiplexed receiver; preserve disabled/muted choices | Launcher regression failed before the fix. Windows wire fixture now exercises parsed capture opt-in with synthetic audio. Physical WASAPI/output-device testing remains necessary. |
| Crackling on Windows receiving Mac audio | PCM capacity/freshness eviction or reset could skip samples without notifying the player; no empty read was required, so the existing underflow ramp was bypassed | Expose discontinuities from `AudioQueue`; both OS players apply the existing 1 ms transition | A virtual device stall reproduced a 24,000-unit single-sample jump before the fix. This establishes a click-producing bug, not the complete cause of the recording. |
| Group video can stop while audio continues | Continuous fresh video could starve requested retransmissions indefinitely; discarding fresh video also discarded pending repairs | Allow one paced repair after at most eight fresh datagrams; preserve repairs when only fresh video expires | Before/after scheduler reproduction: repair still pending after 200 fresh packets before the fix, serviced at slot 8 afterward. An incomplete IDR and its dependent P-chain now recover under continuous fresh traffic. |

Input/control/audio remain ahead of video and repair. Repairs retain their
existing expiry and share the existing wire pacing budget. Audio priming and
freshness limits remain 15 ms and 40 ms; this patch does not mask underruns by
adding a large buffer.

## A freeze is not an expiry limit

There is no fixed group-sharing duration limit. The slow-viewer policy deliberately
pauses a viewer's **video** after sustained pressure (six seconds of bad feedback
or eight seconds at maximum keyframe backoff). Audio remains active. Recovery
probes back off from five to thirty seconds. `SNSHARE_PEER` distinguishes
`active`, `paused`, and `probing`.

The second supplied recording lasts about 43 seconds. Its remote picture appears
unchanged in later sampled frames while sound continues. It does not contain
enough transport/decoder/peer-health counters to distinguish the repair defect,
an intentional congestion pause, and a decoder stall. Phone/compressed recordings
are not end-to-end latency measurements. Private frames and audio were kept out
of this repository.

## Audio diagnostics and an unresolved capture-pause case

The existing one-second viewer health tick now emits `SNV2_AUDIO_OUTPUT_HEALTH`:
`pcmUnderflows`, `pcmCapacityDrops`, `pcmStaleDrops`, `pcmReprimes`, `pcmResets`.
These numeric counters enter bounded connection reports alongside
`SNV2_AUDIO_HEALTH` (`opusPlc`, `opusLate`, `opusErrors`, etc.). Logging stays
outside device callbacks and contains no PCM or device identity.

A normal virtual 16 ms producer/10 ms device schedule remains gap-free. That
cadence alone does not explain the crackling.

A separate virtual-clock experiment found that a 50–80 ms source capture pause
can exhaust the 30 ms PLC allowance. If no old audio was buffered, the next six
new source packets can then be rejected because their sequence numbers were
already concealed, delaying recovery by approximately 40 ms. Rejecting those
numbers is correct when they actually contain delayed old sound. The current
wire contract cannot reliably distinguish these situations. This case is
**not patched**: relaxing sequence/replay checks would reintroduce stale audio.
An explicit source-discontinuity/generation contract needs a separate change.

## Verification and remaining device tests

Regressions cover launcher audio opt-in/disable/mute, PCM continuity after
capacity/stale/reset drops, paced repair fairness, and restoring an IDR/P-chain.
The group audiovisual test now runs three viewers for nine seconds (about
19 Mbps, periodic 256 KB IDRs and dependent P-frames, Opus/PCM and live health
updates), beyond the six-second slow-viewer threshold. Earlier short all-IDR
tests could not establish this behavior.

Synthetic tests do not establish that the exact physical symptoms are gone.
Retest both control directions and group sharing with moving content and sound.
If video freezes, correlate `SNSHARE_PEER` state, completed frames, NACK/repair
and decoder counters. For crackling, compare Opus concealment/late packets with
the new PCM output counters. No release or installed-app replacement is part
of this investigation.
