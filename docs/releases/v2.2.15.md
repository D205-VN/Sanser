# Sanser 2.2.15

Audio playback stability follow-up for ordinary Windows/macOS control sessions.
The reported crackle affects both directions, which use different playback paths.
Update the receiving desktop; updating both desktops is recommended.

- **Windows receiving Mac audio:** replace the 2 ms polling sleep with WASAPI
  device events. Prime output before starting, then refill all available 5 ms
  blocks up to a 20 ms device queue. This avoids starving a 5 ms queue when the
  device period or OS wake-up is longer. A short 15 ms software reserve tolerates
  batched capture callbacks and small arrival jitter, with a bounded tail wait.
- **Mac receiving Windows audio:** bound the hardware queue by duration (20 ms,
  or one larger packet) instead of allowing 48 variable-size buffers to absorb
  the software jitter queue. Wait for playback capacity for at most 25 ms rather
  than immediately dropping the next packet. Do not re-prime the software jitter
  target just because software storage is empty while hardware still has audio.
- **Loss edges:** use a 1 ms transition when silence or an unrelated packet
  replaces lost audio. Continuous valid PCM remains unchanged; non-finite float
  samples are sanitized. This does not reconstruct missing audio or eliminate
  packet loss, clipping at capture, device/driver faults or acoustic feedback.

Validation uses synthetic PCM: signed/endian wire roundtrip, conversion, packet
boundaries, 10 ms capture bursts with arrival jitter, underflow/re-prime/tail
limits, device queue duration, PCM/float discontinuity ramps and authenticated
UDP delivery. Native Mac compilation and the cross-platform CI suite also run.
No physical speaker/microphone capture or listening test is performed by these
fixtures; retest speech/music and Bluetooth/USB outputs on the real two machines.

This patch retains 2.2.14's session/input/Relay changes. Group sharing remains
video-only and view-only; group audio is still not implemented. No claim is made
that the roughly 200 ms route, all physical Share freezes or UAC behavior is fixed.
