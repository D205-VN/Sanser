# Sanser 2.3.30

Direct touch on iPhone and Android now works like ordinary phone apps:

- **Swipe to scroll:** a one-finger swipe scrolls the window under the
  finger on the computer. The content follows the finger (swipe up, the page
  moves up), and a quick flick keeps scrolling for a moment before slowing
  down.
- **Hold then drag** to drag and drop (select text, move files or windows).
  The phone gives a short vibration when the hold is recognised.
- **Hold and lift** to right-click.
- Tap still clicks, and two fingers still zoom and move the picture.
  Touchpad mode is unchanged.

Before this release a one-finger move in direct touch dragged with the left
button held.

Checks: mobile Flutter tests (164) pass, including swipe direction, scroll
position, momentum, hold-to-drag and hold-to-right-click. Not yet tried on
physical phones.

Mobile version: 2.3.30, build 20338.
